# Learning session: what will happen

Thank you for helping. This session tests an app that teaches from a document. It does not test you.

## What we will do (about 45 minutes)

1. A few short questions on paper about two topics, before you learn anything. It is fine to write "I don't know".
2. You learn the first topic for 12 minutes, in one of two ways:
   - **With the app**: the app explains one idea at a time and asks you questions. Answer in your own words. You can press **Hint**, **Explain differently**, or ask your own question. Please say aloud what you are thinking, what confuses you, and what you expect the app to do. I will not help you; if you ask me something, I will ask what you would try.
   - **Reading**: you read the document for the same time, with no other help.
3. You learn the second topic the other way.
4. A few new questions on paper about both topics.
5. A short questionnaire and four questions about the app.

## Your data

- You get a code (for example P3). Your name, email, age and school are not written anywhere.
- Nothing is recorded unless you agree. If you agree, only the screen is recorded, not your face or voice.
- The app keeps the lesson only in this browser tab and it is cleared afterwards.
- The paper sheets carry only your code and are destroyed after the summary is written.
- You can stop at any time, without giving a reason, and what you did so far is not used if you ask.
